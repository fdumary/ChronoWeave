Figma Make Prompt — ChronoWeave Drift Map Screen

Design a mobile UI screen for an app called ChronoWeave that shows a Drift Map, helping users understand how their perception of time changes throughout the day.

The design should follow a playful mental-wellness style with a friendly mascot, soft pastel colors, rounded cards, and no gradients.

Use flat pastel colors, large rounded cards, and generous spacing. The UI should feel calm, friendly, and accessible for neurodivergent users.

Visual Style

Flat design with no gradients

Use pastel colors:

Soft Blue — stable time
Soft Purple — slow or moderate drift
Soft Orange — fast drift
Pale Yellow — UI accents

Background should be very light pastel or off-white.

Use rounded cards, soft shadows, and simple shapes.

Typography should use a rounded sans-serif font with large friendly headings.

Include a small star mascot near the header that reacts to insights.

Screen Title

Your Time Rhythm

Subtitle:

Here’s how time seemed to flow for you today.

Layout Structure

Top section:

Title
Subtitle
Small mascot illustration

Middle section:

Display three large floating cards representing time perception during different parts of the day.

Cards should have soft pastel backgrounds, rounded corners, and subtle shadows.

Cards should appear slightly stacked or floating, inspired by modern playful mobile UI layouts.

Drift Map Cards
Card 1 — Morning

Color: Soft Blue

Title: Morning

State: Stable Time

Description:

Your sense of time feels steady in the morning.

Icon: sun or calm mascot

Card 2 — Afternoon

Color: Soft Purple

Title: Afternoon

State: Moderate Drift

Description:

Time sometimes speeds up or slows down.

Icon: thinking mascot

Card 3 — Evening

Color: Soft Orange

Title: Evening

State: Fast Drift

Description:

Time tends to move quickly in the evening.

Icon: dizzy mascot

Animation & Interaction (Important)

Add playful micro-interactions.

Card Entrance Animation

When the screen loads:

• Cards slide upward from the bottom
• Slight fade-in
• Each card appears with a small delay

Example sequence:

Morning → Afternoon → Evening

Card Hover / Tap Animation

When a card is tapped:

• Card slightly lifts upward (scale 1.03)
• Shadow increases
• A soft pulse ring appears behind the card

Then show a small expandable insight section.

Example expanded insight:

"Many people experience faster time perception during evening screen use."

Mascot Animation

When the screen loads:

• Mascot gently bounces once

When a card is tapped:

• Mascot changes expression based on the selected card

Examples:

Morning → calm smile
Afternoon → thinking face
Evening → dizzy face

Optional Time Rhythm Visualization

Below the cards show a simple horizontal rhythm indicator.

Example concept:

Morning — blue dots
Afternoon — purple dots
Evening — orange dots

This creates a visual representation of time drift.
