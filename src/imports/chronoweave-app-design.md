**Prompt for Figma Make**

Design a mobile app UI prototype for an app called **ChronoWeave**, a tool that helps people with ADHD understand their perception of time (“time blindness”) and improve transitions between tasks.

Use a **playful, friendly visual style inspired by modern mental-wellness apps**, similar to the reference designs with cute character mascots and expressive emotion states. However, **do not use gradients**. Use **flat colors, rounded cards, simple shapes, and soft pastel backgrounds**. The interface should feel calm, supportive, and accessible for neurodivergent users.

### Visual Style

* Flat design with **no gradients**
* Soft pastel palette (lavender, mint, soft blue, pale yellow)
* Rounded UI components and friendly spacing
* Simple cartoon mascot (a small star-shaped character) that changes expression to represent time perception states
* Minimal typography with a clean rounded sans-serif font
* Subtle animations or visual cues like pulses or rings to represent the passage of time
* Avoid clutter; keep screens calm and readable

### Design Language

* Use **character emotions to represent time perception states**
* Use **color-coded cards** to represent different time drift conditions
* Keep messaging supportive and non-judgmental

Examples of states:

* Fast time drift (orange)
* Slow time drift (purple)
* Stable time (blue)
* Transition warning (yellow)

# App User Flow (ChronoWeave)

Design the following **8 screens in order**.

---

### Screen 1 — Intro / Landing

Purpose: Introduce the concept.

Elements:

* App name: ChronoWeave
* Tagline: “Learn how time feels to you.”
* Friendly star mascot
* Start button

Tone: welcoming and calm.

---

### Screen 2 — Time Feeling Selection

Purpose: Understand how the user experiences time.

Question:
“How does time usually feel to you?”

Options displayed as rounded cards with icons and mascots:

⚡ Time flies
🌫 I lose track of time
🐢 Time drags

Button: Continue

---

### Screen 3 — Calibration Test 1

Purpose: Measure initial time estimation.

Interaction:
Large circular button.

Instruction:
“Hold when you think 10 seconds have passed.”

After completion show result card:
Estimated time vs actual time.

Mascot reaction based on result.

---

### Screen 4 — Calibration Test 2

Purpose: Second timing test.

Instruction:
“Tap when you think 30 seconds have passed.”

Show results in a friendly card format.

---

### Screen 5 — Drift Map

Purpose: Show patterns of time perception.

Display simple cards:

Morning — stable
Afternoon — moderate drift
Evening — fast drift

Each card has a small mascot and color coding.

---

### Screen 6 — Today Dashboard

Purpose: Show daily time perception forecast.

Components:

* Title: Today’s Time Rhythm
* Cards showing time stability periods
* Toggle to enable subtle rhythm cues

---

### Screen 7 — Live Nudge

Purpose: Demonstrate the core feature.

Example message:

“Time may be moving quickly right now.”

Options:
Start rhythm cue
Remind me later
Dismiss

Include subtle visual pulse around mascot.

---

### Screen 8 — Weekly Insight

Purpose: Reflect on patterns.

Cards showing insights like:

“Your time runs fastest during evening screen use.”

“Your time estimation improved this week.”

Include a button:
“Run a new calibration test.”

---

### UX Principles

* Encourage curiosity, not correction
* Avoid shame or negative messaging
* Focus on helping users understand their own perception of time
* Keep interactions simple and low effort

The final design should feel like a **supportive companion that helps users develop a better internal sense of time**.
